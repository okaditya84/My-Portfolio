Research & Publications

A patent, papers and applied research.

From a published patent on multimodal healthcare AI to peer-reviewed forecasting and low-resource NLP, my work centres on evaluation, faithfulness and systems that hold up.

Patent · Published

AI-Powered Multimodal Healthcare System for Elderly Care

A multimodal healthcare assistant for elderly users. The core novelty is a unified multimodal vector store that fuses text, voice and image into a single embedding space through an attention-based feature-alignment layer with modality-specific weights, enabling cross-modal retrieval-augmented generation while minimising LLM input cost. A Vision Transformer classifies medical scans, prescriptions and diagnostic reports; speech-to-text, text-to-speech and OCR handle multimodal input; and an adaptive memory personalises advice, medication reminders and lifestyle guidance over time, in English and Hindi.

Indian Patent Application No. 202521086225  ·  Filed 11 Sep 2025  ·  Published 3 Jul 2026  ·  First inventor, with mentor Dr. Debabrata Swain  ·  Pandit Deendayal Energy University

Published

Advanced Forecasting of Solar Power Generation using Bi-LSTM with Fourier Features

A Bi-LSTM with 25 Fourier term-pairs and geo-temporal features for multi-horizon AC-power forecasting across two climatically distinct Indian PV plants, reaching R² = 0.861 and RMSE = 128.09 kW. Adds an adversarial degradation model (soiling, thermal and sensor faults) that quantifies yield loss and generates maintenance recommendations. First author.

Under review · IEEE Transactions

Efficient Labelling of Hinglish Datasets for Hate-Speech Classification

A dual-layered labelling method for low-resource code-mixed Hinglish: an ensemble-consensus pipeline (Twitter-RoBERTa, BETO/mBERT, VADER) with iterative refinement and human feedback bootstraps a high-quality 18K-sentence dataset, reaching 86% accuracy on hate-speech and sentiment classification.

Academic research

Deterministic Document Parsing that Outperforms VLM Parsers

A zero-ML, failure-mode-driven PDF parser reaching CER 0.0135 and token-F1 0.99 on native PDFs, with a self-improving optimizer that lowered character-error rate from 17.07% to 8.29%. Applied research at current work.
